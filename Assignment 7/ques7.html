Typography and Text Styling

The Art of Readable Web Text

Typography plays a vital role in web design. It determines how comfortable and engaging reading feels to users. Designers often balance font size, line height, and letter spacing to achieve harmony and readability. Proper use of hierarchy, weights, and spacing ensures text is visually balanced and easy to follow.

Using contrasting styles for emphasis helps highlight important information. Remember, great typography is not just about choosing fancy fonts—it’s about creating a clear visual rhythm that guides the reader naturally.
